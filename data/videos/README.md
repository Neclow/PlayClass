# Videos

30 overhead recordings of play-behaviour assays in young chickens (days 28 and
29 of rearing) from Oscarsson et al. (2026).

The `videos` directory contains the following subdirectories:

- `day_28/`: 15 recordings (cages C1–C5, groups G1–G3)
- `day_29/`: 15 recordings (cages C1–C5, groups G1–G3)

Filenames follow the pattern
`C{cage}G{group}_Test_{session}_day_{age}_..._Camera_{id}_{timestamp}.mp4`. The
pipeline identifies each video by its shorthand `C{cage}G{group}D{age}` (e.g.
`C1G1D28`).

**Reference:** Oscarsson, R., Hedlund, L., Rutkauskaite, A., & Jensen, P.
(2026). Individual variation in play in young chickens – assessment and
connection to affective state and personality. _Scientific Reports_, 16(1), 634.
<https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-34437-x>
